# Marketing Infrastructure Buildout

## Overview

This project focused on building and organizing the marketing foundation for a B2B company, including external messaging, product collateral, owned media content, website copy, and internal marketing documentation.

## Challenge

The company needed clearer external messaging, more consistent product materials, and a more organized marketing workflow to support public communication, sales enablement, and cross-functional collaboration.

## My Role

As a Marketing Specialist, I supported the development of company-facing and public-facing marketing materials and coordinated with Product, Sales, Delivery/Consulting, design, and vendor teams.

## What I Did

- Built core marketing infrastructure from scratch and standardized external messaging.
- Helped finalize company overview materials for public channels.
- Audited and restructured messaging for existing product lines.
- Created and updated product one-pagers, WeChat articles, and website product copy.
- Built an internal knowledge base for brand, product, and event assets.
- Organized reusable workflows to improve material reuse and cross-team collaboration.

## Outcome

The project improved consistency across public-facing materials, strengthened sales enablement readiness, and created a more organized marketing workflow for future content, product, and event marketing needs.

## Skills Demonstrated

- B2B marketing communications
- Product messaging
- Product collateral
- Sales enablement support
- Cross-functional coordination
- Internal documentation
